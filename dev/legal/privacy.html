Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Parlance is a free German, Latin American Spanish and Italian course made by Matt Colfax, an individual. This page explains what the web version at parlance-iota.vercel.app keeps about you.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: your email address and name. We get nothing else from your Google account.
  • Your learning progress: lessons done, XP, streaks, settings and the notes you choose to send as feedback.
  • If you use Friends: the display name and progress you choose to share with the people you invite.

How it is used

Only to run the course: to sign you in, save your progress so it follows you between devices, and improve the lessons. We don’t show ads, we don’t sell your data and we don’t share it with anyone for marketing.

Where it is stored

In a Supabase database, and in your own browser. The site is hosted on Vercel. Speaking practice uses your device’s microphone only while you hold it open, and audio isn’t stored.

Your choices

You can use Parlance without signing in; progress then stays on your device. To see or delete your account and everything saved with it, email the address below and it will be removed.

Questions? Email mattcolfax@gmail.com.
