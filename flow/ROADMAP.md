# Roadmap

The flow's backlog. The planner picks from it each cycle (after approved proposals), adds what it notices, and keeps the table's shape: `| ID | Area | Idea | Why / evidence | Status |`. Rows are roughly in priority order; the planner may reorder. Matt can add, edit, reorder or delete rows any time; a row he deletes or marks `parked` is never planned.

Areas: `content`, `stories`, `lesson-types`, `ux`, `pictures`, `quality`, `perf`. Statuses: `open`, `planned`, `done (<version>)`, `needs-matt` (Red: waiting on him; see flow/POLICY.md), `parked`.

**Premium program** ([PREMIUM.md](PREMIUM.md)): seven releases are already planned. P1 is `flow/plans/2026-11-06-p1-your-level-your-pace.md`; P2 to P7 wait in `flow/plans/queue/` (P2 works offline, P3 keep going, P4 play, P5 listen, P6 conversation, P7 your week). When no plan is waiting, the planner **promotes the next queued plan** (move it to `flow/plans/<cycle>-<name>.md`, set Release, Base and the cycle, check it still matches `main`, fix only what has drifted) instead of planning from scratch. Rows marked `planned (P<n>)` belong to it.
